# Building

Rebuilding this site in Astro, moving off the SvelteKit version. Most of the work is unglamorous (the SEO layer, the bilingual routing, the markdown pipeline), but it is the part that decides whether writing here stays pleasant.

Kapturae is the other thing taking time. A browser-only photobooth: capture, compose, export, nothing uploaded. The capture and export paths work; templates and print layout are still moving.

# Running

A small VPS with rootless Podman and systemd user units. Everything I self-host lives there, deployed from GitHub Actions over SSH. Most of what I have written this year came out of running it and finding out what actually breaks.

# Learning

Reading more about how browsers schedule work: layout, paint, and the parts of a page that cost nothing until they suddenly cost everything. Also spending time with SQLite in places I would previously have reached for Postgres, and being surprised how far it goes.
